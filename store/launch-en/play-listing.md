**Full description:**

For a hundred nights, the sun has not risen over Aurelle. The Hollow King shattered the Dawnstone and buried its five Embers in the Depths. Take up the Last Lantern, descend into the dark, and bring the dawn home.

FALL. RISE. RETURN STRONGER.
Fast portrait action made for one thumb. Dodge through danger, strike with your weapon, unleash your hero’s skill, and charge a screen-clearing Flare. Every run leads deeper—and every choice can change the route ahead.

FIND YOUR WAY THROUGH THE DEPTHS
Travel across five Depths and eight regions, from Whisperwood and the Sunken Crypt to the Crystal Hollows and the Hollow Throne. Face dangerous elites, monsters, and six multi-phase bosses. Choose your next step through branching rooms: fight, rest, visit a shrine, uncover treasure, or take a chance on a mysterious event.

MAKE EVERY RUN YOUR OWN
Discover more than 85 spirit Boons from six elemental Spirits. Mix fire, ice, storm, poison, light, and shadow. Find powerful Duo and Legendary Boons, collect relics, and combine upgrades into a build that changes how you fight.

A WORLD TO RETURN TO
Come back to Emberhollow between descents. Meet its villagers, follow a 15-chapter story, take on side quests, and prepare for what waits below. Choose from four heroes with distinct weapons and signature skills: Rowan, Sera, Elio, and Kaito. Find gear, forge upgrades, unlock permanent talents at the Lantern Tree, and grow stronger with every journey.

MADE FOR MOBILE
• One-thumb portrait controls, responsive dashing, and optional auto-attack.
• Offline play with automatic saves—no Dawnbound account required.
• Five Depths, eight regions, four heroes, branching runs, and six bosses.
• Optional rewarded videos can grant a revive or an in-game bonus.
• A one-time Remove Ads purchase hides banner and full-screen ads. Rewarded videos remain optional.
