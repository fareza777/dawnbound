- Title: Remove Ads
- Description: Removes banner and full-screen ads permanently. Optional rewarded videos remain available.

## Data safety reference

- Ads declaration: Yes, the app contains ads.
- The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share device or other identifiers, general location derived from IP, app interactions, and diagnostics for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. Data is encrypted in transit.
- Game progress is saved locally. Dawnbound does not operate an account system or game server.
- Google Play Billing processes purchases; the game receives purchase status to provide or restore the purchased product.
- Deletion: clear the app's storage or uninstall it to delete local saves. Reset the advertising ID in Android settings.
- Audience: not directed to children under 13.
- Content rating: fantasy action/violence, no blood; no user-generated content or real-money gambling.
