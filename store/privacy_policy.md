# Dawnbound — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 24 September 2026_

Dawnbound ("the game") is a single-player game. This policy explains what data is involved when you play.

**Your game progress:** saved only on your device. It never leaves your device and is removed when you uninstall the game or clear its app data. We do not run servers and we do not have accounts.

**Advertising (free version):** the game shows ads provided by **Google AdMob**. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process:
- your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers,
- approximate location derived from your IP address,
- ad interactions and basic app/device information (such as diagnostics).

In the EEA, the UK and other regions where it is required, the game asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time in **Settings → Ad Privacy Choices**. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings. Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy — how Google uses data from partner apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

**Purchases:** the optional "Remove Ads" purchase is processed entirely by **Google Play**. We never see your payment details. Google Play tells the game only whether the purchase is active, so it can be restored on your devices.

**We do not** sell personal data, and the game itself does not collect personal information such as your name, email or contacts.

**Permissions:** internet access (ads and purchases), the advertising ID (ads), billing (purchases) and vibration (haptics). The game does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.

**Children:** Dawnbound is not directed at children under 13 and is not designed to collect information from them.
