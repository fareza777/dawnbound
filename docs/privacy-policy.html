Privacy Policy

For Dawnbound: Roguelike RPG
Last updated: September 26, 2026

This policy explains what information is used when you play Dawnbound. The game is published by F7 Developer (“we,” “us,” or “our”).

Game progress and account information

We do not operate a Dawnbound account system or game server. Your game progress and settings are saved on your device. We do not receive that local save data. You can delete it by clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling the game. Google Play may process purchase information when you buy an in-game product.

Advertising and information used by Google

The free version includes ads provided by Google AdMob. To deliver, measure, and protect its advertising services, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share information with Google, including your IP address (which may be used to estimate a general location), app interactions such as launches and ad views, diagnostic and performance information, and device or other identifiers such as the Android advertising ID. Google may use this information for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. Data sent by the SDK is encrypted in transit.

Where required, Google’s consent tools ask for your choices before using personal data for advertising. You can review or change available choices in the game under Settings → Ad Privacy Choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings. See Google’s Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

In-app purchases

Optional purchases, including Remove Ads, are handled by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your full payment-card details. The game receives purchase status so that it can provide or restore the item you bought. Google handles purchase data under its own privacy terms.

Permissions and sharing

The game uses an internet connection for advertising and purchases and vibration for haptics. It does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, or precise location. We do not sell personal information. Information collected by Google’s SDK is shared with Google as described above.

Children’s privacy

Dawnbound is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us so we can address it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game or its data practices change. The latest version will always be available at this page, with its update date shown above.
